FOLDER 02 — BUILD YOUR AGENT TONIGHT (start here)
=================================================
Open the files in this order:

1. WORKSHEET-build-your-loan-agent.md
     Your complete step-by-step guide. It contains the four
     instruction blocks to copy word-for-word, Paths A/B/C,
     and the deployment guide. Everything else supports it.

2. application_priya.txt
     The practice loan application you ATTACH to your agent.
     (Priya is invented — every applicant in this kit is.)

3. eligibility_rules.txt
     The 8 rules your agent checks. You PASTE this into the
     agent's instructions where the worksheet says RULES.

4. golden_set.txt
     Five test cases with the correct answers. After building,
     run all five. Five greens = your agent works.
     Test 5 matters most: a good agent STOPS on contradictions.

Time: ~20 minutes to build, about an hour with testing.
Cost: zero. Tool: any free AI assistant (the worksheet shows how).
