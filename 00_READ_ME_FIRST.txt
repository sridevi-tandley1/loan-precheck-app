PERSONAL LOAN PRE-CHECK RULES  (Training exercise - synthetic data only)

R1  AGE          : 21 to 60 years.
R2  INCOME       : Net monthly income at least Rs 25,000.
R3  LOAN SIZE    : Requested amount not more than 20 times net monthly income.
R4  CREDIT SCORE : 700 or above = pass. 650-699 = NEEDS HUMAN REVIEW. Below 650 = not eligible at this time.
R5  DOCUMENTS    : All four required - ID proof, Address proof, 3 salary slips, 6-month bank statement.
R6  AFFORDABILITY: Estimated new EMI = loan amount / tenure months (simplified - ignore interest).
                   Existing EMIs + new EMI must not exceed 50% of net monthly income.
R7  CONSISTENCY  : If any two pieces of information do not match (e.g. stated income vs salary slips), NEEDS HUMAN REVIEW.
R8  AUTHORITY    : The agent RECOMMENDS ONLY. It never approves and never rejects. A loan officer decides.
